FAQ

Common Questions

What is AI Visibility and why does it matter for dentists?

AI Visibility refers to how well your dental practice appears in AI-generated search results — including Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and voice assistants. With 2.5 billion monthly AI Overview users and 60% of searches ending without a click, being visible in AI results is now essential for attracting new patients.

How is this different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO focuses on ranking in Google's list of links. AI Visibility optimization (also called GEO/AEO) ensures your practice is recommended by AI systems that generate answers directly. It builds on SEO but adds entity resolution, structured data, and AI-specific content optimization.

What does the free audit include?

We test your practice across multiple AI platforms — ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, and more — documenting exactly how each AI describes and recommends (or doesn't recommend) your practice. You receive a detailed report with specific findings and recommendations.

How long until I see results?

Entity resolution and schema fixes can show improvements within 2-4 weeks. Full AI visibility optimization typically shows significant results within 60-90 days, with continuous improvement as AI systems re-index your optimized content.

Do I need to replace my current SEO provider?

No. AI Visibility Pro complements your existing SEO. We focus specifically on the AI layer — making sure your practice is understood and recommended by AI systems. Your SEO provider handles traditional rankings; we handle AI recommendations.
